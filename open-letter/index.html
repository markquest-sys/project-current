Dear Friends and Future Partners,

What if the opportunity that changes a young person's life begins with something as simple as being given the chance to try?

To hold a tool.Solve a problem.Build something real.Learn from someone who knows the work.

For many young people, the question isn't whether they have potential. It's whether they'll have the opportunity to discover it.

That's why we're building PROJECT :: CURRENT.

Beginning in Santa Clarita, we're creating opportunities for young people to work alongside skilled professionals, employers, and community partners. Not to tell them what they should become, but to help them discover what they can do.

And we're inviting you to help make that possible.

First funding milestone
$25,000 The founding floor. A first milestone, not the full pilot budget.

We're seeking founding benefactors, organizations, and individuals willing to contribute financial support, equipment, expertise, introductions, or practical resources.

  • Financial support
  • Equipment
  • Expertise
  • Introductions
  • Practical resources

Every contribution has a purpose. Every relationship can open a door.

Our Open Standard

We intend to be transparent about the support we receive, the resources we need, and the progress we make together.

  1. 01Make the work visible.
  2. 02Make participation possible.
  3. 03Make progress accountable.

We'll openly share what we need, what people commit, what we receive, and what we accomplish. We'll listen, learn, and adapt as we go.

Because meaningful progress doesn't come from money alone. It comes from combining what each of us can contribute.

One person's knowledge.Another's resources.Someone else's connection.A young person's curiosity.

Individually, each has value. Together, they create possibilities none could produce alone.

The invitation is simple. See what we're building. Consider what you might contribute. And if the purpose resonates, start a conversation with us.

Visit projectcurrent.org ↗

Somewhere in our community is a young person who hasn't yet discovered what they're capable of.

Not because the potential isn't there.Because the opportunity hasn't been.

The right people, resources, and opportunities can change that. And that's what we're bringing together.
